# Hi, I'm Erica 👋

## Operations, data, and automation

I work at the intersection of **operations, technology, and business improvement**.

My current day job is in pharmaceutical operations, but the work I enjoy most goes beyond keeping things running. I find the friction in a process, understand why it exists, turn messy information into something useful, and build a clearer, more scalable way of working.

I’m particularly interested in:

- Operations strategy and process improvement
- Data analysis and operational reporting
- Workflow automation
- Supply chain and logistics
- Customer and commercial operations
- Building better systems from imperfect processes
- Helping teams make decisions with reliable data

I like practical problems, ambiguous starting points, and projects where a spreadsheet, a repeated manual task, or a broken handover can become the starting point for something much better.

## What I bring

### Turning operational problems into systems

I am comfortable working close to the detail while keeping sight of the bigger picture. That means understanding how a process actually works in practice, identifying where time and quality are lost, and working with technical teams to improve the underlying system.

I have worked on projects involving:

- Order management and fulfilment workflows
- Customer onboarding
- Logistics and transport networks
- Cost-to-serve analysis
- Rate cards and commercial modelling
- Inventory and supply risk
- Customer support categorisation
- Operational KPIs and business reviews
- Credit control and order-to-cash processes
- Contracts negotiation

### Using data to make operations clearer

I enjoy taking fragmented operational data and turning it into something people can use.

That includes building trackers, defining useful metrics, improving reporting logic, working with Excel and SQL-based analysis, and helping teams move from manually maintained reports towards more reliable, centralised data.

The goal is not simply to produce another dashboard. The goal is to help people answer better questions:

- Where are we losing time?
- What is creating unnecessary cost?
- Which customers, products, or markets need attention?
- Is the process working consistently?
- What should we automate next?

### Designing for scale

Some of my favourite projects have started with a simple question:

> How do we make this work better without adding more manual effort?

I have worked on workflow improvements that reduced order-handling time, replaced fragile spreadsheet processes with more structured systems, improved data quality at the point of entry, and created a stronger foundation for automation.

I care about building processes that are:

- Clear enough for someone new to follow
- Reliable enough for a team to trust
- Measurable enough to improve
- Flexible enough to grow with the business


## Selected work

### Operations workflow automation

Helped move a manually maintained order-tracking process towards a structured workflow with validation, centralised data, and automated downstream steps.

The result was a more reliable operating model with:

- Faster order entry and customer notifications
- Faster preparation of orders for fulfilment
- Fewer manual checks and duplicated updates
- Better visibility across teams
- A foundation for future invoicing, fulfilment, and customer communication automation

### Logistics and cost optimisation

Worked on transport analysis, cost-to-serve modelling, country-level logistics assumptions, and rate-card development.

One project reduced average transport cost per parcel in Spain from approximately €150 to €75. The wider work involved comparing actual transport costs, third-party rate cards, shipment profiles, service requirements, and commercial assumptions.

### Operational reporting and KPI design

Helped develop operational reporting around measures such as:

- Order-processing lead time
- Delivery performance
- On-time-in-full performance
- Transport cost
- Defect handling
- Customer support resolution time
- Credit control and outstanding receivables
- Inventory availability

I’m interested in the thinking behind metrics as much as the reporting itself: whether the metric is well-defined, whether the data is trustworthy, and whether the result leads to a useful decision.

### Customer and service operations

Built and improved categorisation approaches for customer support tickets, creating better visibility into ticket types, customer needs, workload, and resource allocation.

This kind of work sits between customer experience, operations, data quality, and process design. It is often where small structural improvements create a meaningful difference for both customers and teams.


## How I work

I tend to be the person who:

- Asks why a process works the way it does
- Notices when a recurring task should probably be automated
- Turns vague problems into structured work
- Documents the reasoning, not just the result
- Cares about data accuracy and operational detail
- Connects people who understand the problem with people who can build the solution
- Looks for improvements that save time without reducing quality
- Enjoys making complicated things easier to understand

I am not interested in technology for its own sake. I am interested in using technology, data, and good process design to help teams work better.
